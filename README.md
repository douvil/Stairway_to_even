This python script only need three netcdf input files corresponding over three annual mean timeseries averaged over a selected region over land (here global land areas).
The idea is to represent climate change as an idealized staircase where each step represents a 30-year timeslice with annual warming stripes (in K) in the front and annual precipitation stripes (mm/day) on the top. The right side shows annual stripes for potential evapotranspiration (PET) over the whole 1901-2024 period of observations. All stripes represent annual mean global land anomalies from the CRU TS4 dataset relative to the 1901-1930 climatology. They illustrate both an obvious increase in surface temperature and precipitation, potentially leading to more heatwaves and floods at the global scale, but also a continuous increase in PET favouring the occurrence of hydrological and agricultural droughts.
